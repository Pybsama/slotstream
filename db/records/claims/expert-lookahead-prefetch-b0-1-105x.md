The first held-out B0 cohort measured an aggregate 1.105x over the previous default, after the true-median correction. This is prefetch with slot adoption, on one 48 GB M5 Pro at a 20 GB target with two drafts. The later combined B1 configuration used different prompts, so subtracting the two ratios does not isolate its additions. This guide first brings the corrected B0 result onto a public documentation surface; it is not a new benchmark.
