Opened on 2026-09-14 from the closing profiles of the decode serialization plan ([[records/measurements/decode-path-serialization-closing-profiles-2026-09-13]]), which leave file reads as the largest cost in decode and name forecast accuracy at the same lead time as the lever, after round 2 showed that more speculative reads do not reduce demand reads ([[records/measurements/decode-path-serialization-round-2-2026-09-12]]).

### Problem

The shipped forecast for target layer T applies T's own hyper-connection read and router to the streams after layer T-2's MoE add. Between deferred barriers it is evaluated with layer T-1's routing readback and reaches the scheduler there ([[records/measurements/decode-path-serialization-round-3b-2026-09-13]]). At that readback the streams already hold T-1's attention output, so a forecast built on them arrives just as early and misses only T-1's routed experts and T's attention sublayer, instead of two whole layers. T-1's shared expert is resident and needs no routing, so it can be added too.

### Taps

`RouterForecastTap`: `boundary` (shipped: after a source layer's MoE add, at a stride), `attention` (the streams of T-1 after its attention add, riding T-1's routing readback) and `attention-shared` (the same plus T-1's shared expert, whose matmuls T-1's MoE call then reuses). A router policy on an attention tap issues its candidates on arrival. The capture command records any tap without a scheduler, and shard format 3 carries the tap in the forecast record.

Exit: exact outputs and, for the chosen tap, a paired ratio of at least 1.01 with at least 8 of 12 pairs above 1, before B1 is run. 5. Co-routing prior, offline. When T-1's routing readback returns, the host also holds T-1's routed experts. 6. Learned correction, offline. Steps 5 and 6 need no native timing and run whenever no timed run is active; neither waits for step 3.

Exit, over targets 2 to 47: the native corrected top-10 set equals the offline one in at least 99% of rows, native agreement within 0.005 of 0.7980 and the plain tap within 0.002 of 0.7292, before any timing. ### Limits

One machine. The twin counts reads, not in-flight joins or evictions by wasted reservations, so its projections are not claims. The screen's reference arm is the explicit qualified configuration: with the draft head forced on below its activation floor, automatic mode runs without the lookahead, so the default itself cannot be the reference at a forced profile. The correction is derived data for one model checkpoint, trained on the pilot's training requests; shipping it needs its own decision about where the file lives and how it is reproduced. The confirmation set has no multilingual prompt.

### Closed (2026-09-16)

The corrected attention tap ships as the default in 0.2.19 ([[records/decisions/corrected-decode-forecast-default-with-the-sidecar]]): the correction file is an optional pinned sidecar of the model mirror that `pull` fetches next to the weights, the engine locates it there and charges 409 MiB, and `SLOTSTREAM_EXPERT_PREFETCH_TAP=boundary` keeps the previous forecast. The measurements are recorded under [[records/measurements/decode-forecast-taps-2026-09-15]] and the shipping build's own default-path benchmark under [[records/measurements/corrected-forecast-release-benchmark-2026-09-16]]. Levers closed on their registered readings: the shared-expert variant (by rule), the co-routing prior, the lower issue threshold and the attention readout in both placements. What the program leaves open is outside the forecast: about 64% of decode time is GPU compute of the verify passes at about 2.1 accepted tokens per pass, so the next levers are acceptance per pass, kernel time per pass and the attention cost at long contexts.
