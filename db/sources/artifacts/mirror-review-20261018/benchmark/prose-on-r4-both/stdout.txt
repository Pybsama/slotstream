The narrative of the coastal observatory serves as a profound allegory for robust software engineering and scientific data integrity, illustrating that reliability often stems from respecting the nuances of data rather than blindly trusting automated systems. The first and perhaps most critical lesson concerns the semantic distinction between missing data and zero values. In many computational contexts, developers default to filling missing values with zeros to prevent crashes or simplify calculations. However, as Mara’s ledger demonstrates, this practice can catastrophically distort reality. A "calm wind" is a physical measurement of zero velocity, whereas a "missing observation" indicates a failure in the sensing mechanism, such as a disconnected cable
