Capture policy

These are the recorded text/JSON receipts. Local home, checkout, model and
evidence paths use neutral labels. Commands, numerical results, token IDs,
generated text, timestamps and build hashes are otherwise unchanged.
`capture-manifest.json` records both the original and published SHA-256 and
size for every file, and flags path relabelling. The original capture manifest
remains available as `original-capture-manifest.json`.

No checkpoint payload, tensor binary, executable, cache, device serial number,
private hostname or credential is included. Hardware and backing disk facts
are in `hardware-storage.json`; the physical-device proof is also retained in
the benchmark's `disk-topology.json`. Original private captures remain local.
